## A. Personnel — Federal $0 / Non-Federal $1,290,000

No Iowa DOT salaries are charged to Federal funds. The non-Federal amount is
in-kind staff time: **eight Iowa DOT positions at 0.25 FTE each** (no
individual exceeds quarter-time; approximately 10 hours per week per person)
for 48 months, valued at actual payroll plus fringe per 2 CFR 200.306(i) and
documented by timesheets against a dedicated project code. Positions and
fully-loaded annual values (state classification maximums, FY27 pay plan;
values true up to actual incumbent payroll):

| Position | Classification | Loaded annual |
|---|---|---:|
| Corridor program lead | Executive Officer 3 (grade 35) | $174,836 |
| Construction office engineer — I-35 field verification | Transportation Engineer Senior (grade 37) | $190,270 |
| District field engineer — I-80 field verification | Transportation Engineer Senior (grade 37) | $190,270 |
| GIS/IT integration engineer | IT Specialist 5 (grade 35) | $174,836 |
| Data analytics engineer — prediction, calibration, evaluation support | IT Specialist 5 (grade 35) | $174,836 |
| TMC operations lead | Program Planner 3 | $129,060 |
| Traffic data coordinator — ground truth & reporting | Program Planner 3 | $129,060 |
| Grants administration & executive oversight | Program Planner 3 | $129,060 |

Sum × 0.25 × 4 years = $1,292,228, booked conservatively at **$1,290,000**.

**Substitution flexibility:** with FHWA approval, Iowa DOT may substitute
equivalent university-partner staff (Iowa State University Institute for
Transportation) and/or partner state DOT staff for a portion of this
commitment at equal or greater certified value; the total non-Federal share
will not be reduced. Partner contributions certified after submission will
be added as voluntary committed cost share.

## D. Equipment — Federal $1,600,000

Field systems with unit cost ≥$5,000: connected work zone devices (smart

## F. Contractual — Federal $7,300,000 / Non-Federal $1,400,000

All services procured competitively per 2 CFR 200.319–320. No provider is
named in this budget; quotes obtained during planning are retained as
market-price documentation. Composition:

1. **Data and technology service categories — $5,000,000:** exchange
   backbone and AI infrastructure for verification/prediction ($2,000,000);
   in-cab alert network integration and dissemination ($1,200,000);
   connected-vehicle movement analytics and derived datasets ($1,000,000);
   V2X/TIM message distribution services ($500,000); fleet telematics/ELD
   data services ($300,000).
2. **Program-support services — $1,500,000 (15.6% of Federal request):**
   program management and 16-state coordination support (~0.75 FTE),
   systems engineering and ARC-IT documentation (Year-1 concentrated),
   procurement support, Federal reporting and grant administration, and
   outreach/toolkit delivery. Individual consultant compensation is subject
   to the daily rate limitation in the agency's general terms.
3. **Platform integration and operations services — $800,000.**

The non-Federal $1,400,000 is third-party in-kind contributed by data and
technology partners against contractual scope, documented by commitment
letters certifying dollar value, valuation basis (2 CFR 200.306),
contribution schedule, and non-Federal funding source: AI
sandbox/exchange-infrastructure partner $700,000; movement-analytics
partner $300,000; message-distribution partner $200,000; fleet-telematics
partner $200,000 (one-time contribution within a $500,000 service
arrangement of which $300,000 is grant-funded). Partner cost-share offers
may be included as evaluation criteria in competitive selections, open to
all bidders.
