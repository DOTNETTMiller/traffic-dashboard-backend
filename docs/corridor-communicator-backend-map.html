How it worked before

the problem this is answering

Every state a closed loop: its own 511, its own ATMS, its own TMC, its own format. Nothing checked whether a reported zone was real, geometry was a straight line between two points, and what reached a driver was late, partial, or never arrived. The figures below are measured on the live public feeds, not asserted.

What was trueMeasuredWhat changed
No location on most events A usable coordinate existed on 1,505 of 6,226 events. Camera matching returned null for the other 76% before it ever looked for a camera. Matching runs against the zone's whole line, not a centroid it may not have.
Geometry was a straight line Two points and the line between them — across rivers, through interchanges, on the wrong carriageway. Road-following linework, full length, both carriageways when both are closed. Gated; unchanged if every tier fails.
Nothing verified a zone was real The feed asserted it; the only check was a person looking at a camera. Fixed cameras capped verification at 52 closures. Five independent witnesses, plus the maintenance fleet's own dashcams covering roads no fixed camera sees.
Devices were linked by hand Connected arrow boards were manually associated by TMC staff with 511 events, one at a time. Automatic multi-signal association on route, direction and along-road chainage.
Identifiers moved silently One federally-registered feed re-identified 6 of ~1,050 events in 18 hours, destroying 39 ids of zones that never left. Continuity keyed on the observed-stable core, with the drift monitored rather than assumed away.
A published feed could be empty A state's registered endpoint answering HTTP 200 with an HTML error page — healthy to any status-code check. Health is content-level: parseable, expected shape, records present.
Coverage stopped at the state line ~17 states publish no public work-zone or 511 feed at all. Nationwide weather alerting fills those gaps; builders let a jurisdiction author a feed without a platform.
Nobody knew what reached drivers Publication was assumed to be arrival. No measurement either way. Deviation scorecard buckets every zone as reaching, not reaching, mistimed or unreported — and routes each to a fix.
The consequence was circular.

Downstream consumers could not rely on the data, so they did not integrate deeply. Because they did not integrate, nobody found out which zones failed to reach anyone — and there was no signal telling a state what to fix. Everything after this point exists to break that loop.

01

The spine

ingest → identify → corroborate → repair → publish

One adapter per publisher, so every defect is attributable to the adapter that produced it. The event cache is the shared object everything else reads; subsystems annotate it in place rather than forking their own copy.

Every check reports one of three states.

Pass, fail, or not evaluated — never rendered as pass. No credentials, cooldown, unreachable and unparseable are four different answers, and a feed returning HTTP 200 with an error page is not healthy.

02

Corroboration

five independent witnesses

Each witness originates in a different operational chain from the DOT feed making the claim. Positive-only and sticky — corroboration accumulates and is never demoted, because most witnesses can confirm presence and none can prove absence. Exactly one may demote.

Association is multi-signal, not nearest-device. A connected arrow board is almost never at the zone — it sits upstream in the travel direction, often half a mile ahead, and two carriageways sit about 100 ft apart. Chainage on the state LRS turns straight-line distance into true along-road distance; a miss keeps the straight-line figure rather than mixing the two.

A wrong polyline is worse than an honest straight line.

The straight line advertises that it is an approximation; the wrong one does not. Correct geometry decides which structures a zone interacts with, is what validation runs against, and is what mapping partners need to place a closure and suggest a sensible alternative route.

Rail & grade crossings

A raw position carries roughly 1.8 miles of uncertainty at median speed and age — a circle that wide overlaps dozens of unrelated crossings. Snapping to track turns it into a one-dimensional interval that overlaps only the crossings actually ahead.

The geofence is on the equipment, not on the road.

Sign activation is normally event-driven and static: an event fires a rule, the nearest sign lights. Here the fence travels with the hazard — a combine, a plow, an arrow board, a TMA — and the question asked continuously is which fixed message devices are inside it now. As the asset moves the matched set changes: signs ahead pick the message up, signs behind release it. The fence is asymmetric for the same reason a taper is, weighted ahead of travel because that is where drivers are approaching from.

Nothing about it is specific to farm equipment. Any asset that already reports position can carry one, which is what makes an ordinary AVL or telematics feed into driver-facing warning at scale — with no operator watching a map, and no new field hardware.

Operating discipline

why it stays cheap and honest
RuleHow it shows up
Lazy, not scheduledCamera inventories fetch on first use; vision runs per request, never in a loop; partner feeds fill when a layer is opened; device matching is on-demand with a 3-minute reuse window and a concurrency guard. An idle dashboard makes no requests.
Budget · breaker · cacheAll three on every external call, with a self-imposed daily ceiling below the provider's own so the guardrail survives their terms changing.
Ledgers bound costOnly due work runs. Cost is independent of call frequency, and a non-detection never consumes a check.
Typed degradationUnavailable reports unavailable — not empty, not zero. Services ship uncredentialed and return a reason rather than throwing.
Durable stateSQLite locally, Postgres/PostGIS in production by one environment variable. Application disk is ephemeral; a sticky ledger that evaporates on deploy is not sticky.
Source of recordAuthoritative source first; mirrors kept as declared fallbacks, never silently substituted.
Self-contained toolsThe 51 distributed builders connect only to that jurisdiction's own public services — never back here. They work offline.
Never synthesisedWorker presence, restrictions, lane counts, organisation names, update timestamps. An unfillable field gets a placeholder that cannot be mistaken for data.
Counts read from the tree: 71 service modules totalling 21,792 lines, 504 API endpoints across 87 groups, 65 tables, 254 external endpoints. The platform does not mint connected-vehicle messages, operate roadside units or distribute to vehicles — it produces better source feeds in existing federal formats for the distributors that do. The 132-component operator interface and the 51 distributed jurisdiction builders are separate surfaces.
