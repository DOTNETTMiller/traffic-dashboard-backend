What counts as evidence. Only sources carrying live telemetry can validate a work zone. Two of the device sources — PennDOT TSAMS and MaineDOT — are static GIS asset inventories with no message field and no timestamp. Their 853 records are ingested for coverage reporting but are explicitly excluded from adjudication: a row saying a sign exists nearby can neither confirm a zone nor mark it stale.
