■ What Iowa is proposing

A multi-state project on the I-35 and I-80 corridors that brings public and private data together for the two problems drivers feel most: work zones that appear without warning, and nowhere to park at the end of a shift. States keep publishing exactly what they publish today — no new systems, no new staff. The project layers independent evidence on top, verifies every work zone, predicts parking availability at every facility hourly, and delivers one trusted feed to the places drivers already look.

■ Why Iowa leads credibly

  • The platform is operational today — ingesting ~39 work zone feeds nationally, with parking predictions calibrated against Iowa's rest-area camera network.
  • Iowa is the geographic and institutional hub — the two corridors physically meet here, and Iowa anchors the AASHTO-coordinated corridor initiative.
  • The coalition scales because the ask is small — partner states join by letter of commitment; private providers are selected competitively after award.
