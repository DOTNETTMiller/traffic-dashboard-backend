reserve. If the period is cut to 36 months at award negotiation, staff match
drops ~$323k and the frame goes ~$45k underwater — convert InTrans in that
scenario.

**non-federal sources** (university/state funds) and not already committed
as cost-share to another award. Book via letter of commitment or
subrecipient agreement certifying value, rates, and funding source.

Rules: competitive selection (Iowa DOT professional services process);
individual-consultant daily rate cap per FHWA grant terms {verify clause};
**organizational conflict of interest** — any firm that assisted with this
application should clear OCI with Iowa DOT procurement before bidding
(disclosure/firewall usually sufficient; resolve now, not at protest).

**Consultant time as match:** *paid* consultant time is a project COST,
never match. Consultant time counts as match ONLY as **donated (unbilled)
third-party in-kind services** per 2 CFR 200.306(b) — valued at the firm's
normal rates, certified by letter (value, hours, rates, non-federal source),
tracked like all in-kind, and never also billed. A firm may donate hours as
cost-share on its own competitively-won contract (same structure as the
Geotab-type deal). Application-preparation time is never allowable as cost
or match. Any such donation books as ADDITIONAL cushion on top of the
$2,798,000 — do not count it until certified.
