Work-Zone Request Builders — all 50 states and DC

A live functionality demo. Pick a state, draw a work zone on the map, and watch it auto-snap the milepost/route from that state's own public GIS, check bridge clearance (NBI) and lane counts, build the MUTCD taper, flag detour & clearance impacts, and export a standards-compliant WZDx v4.2 feature. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is submitted or stored anywhere.

Each builder connects only to its state DOT's public GIS plus national NBI (bridge clearance) and NHS (lane counts) — no central backend. Demo mode hides the optional data-submission fields; the WZDx / JSON / PDF exports are all client-side.
